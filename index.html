How I operate

Discovery → Handoff
  1. 01

    Discover onsite

    Sit with the client's team, watch the real workflow, and find where a scripted human process becomes a deterministic conversation flow.

  2. 02

    Design the flow

    State-driven conversation logic and structured prompts — built for the failure modes of a regulated, high-stakes call, not a demo.

  3. 03

    Integrate live

    Wire into the client's actual stack — CRM, telephony, webhooks, internal APIs — including hand-off to a human agent when needed.

  4. 04

    Evaluate & iterate

    Audit real calls and transcripts against baseline metrics, diagnose regressions, and A/B test flow changes before they ship wider.
